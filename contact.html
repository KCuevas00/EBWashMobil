Got Questions?

Fleet Wash FAQ

Here are quick answers to the most common questions fleet dispatchers and owner-operators ask us.

Do we need to supply water or power on our lot?

No. Our mobile wash units are 100% self-contained with large-capacity onboard water tanks and commercial gas pressure washers. We can wash in gravel lots, remote yards, or commercial terminals with zero connections.

Can you wash our fleet while drivers are on rest?

Yes, absolutely. We often schedule fleet cleaning over weekends, early mornings, or during scheduled 10-hour rest breaks so your equipment is spotless without losing a single hour of driving time.

Are your detergents safe for vehicle decals and aluminum?

Yes. We use premium commercial-grade vehicle soaps and neutral cleaners that strip diesel grime and salt without fading vinyl logos or oxidizing polished aluminum tanks and wheels.

Do you offer recurring fleet contract discounts?

Yes! We offer discounted rates for recurring accounts (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) and volume discounts for yards with 5+ units washed during the same visit.
